Layer 2 · Learn as you build

Training mode teaches you as you build.

What we foundA first-timer on our team took about 22 minutes and 15 messages to make her first save. Two GitHub tokens were pasted by accident.
So we built training mode:
  • Hover anything to see what it means.
  • A one-minute “Start here” tour.
  • A failed save shows up with a one-click fix.
  • Pasted passwords get blocked.
